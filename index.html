SHEET 4

Phased Rollout

1
Month 1 · Foundation

Get found and get organized — no spend yet

  • Website rebuilt for local SEO and GEO: schema markup, 40–60 word answer blocks, consistent name/address/phone everywhere
  • One CRM, connected to a sequencing agent — a single stack, not five tools stitched together
2
Month 1–2 · Data & Lists

Stand up the feeds, decide on MLS

  • Turn on the free Ramsey + Hennepin feeds (Sheet 1) — live today
  • Decide on Northstar MLS access — it's the one paid call that unlocks the realtor BD list and pre‑sale timing
  • Build teardown‑candidate scoring into the feed (validated in Saint Paul)
3
Month 2+ · Outbound (ongoing)

Personalized mail, then realtor relationships

  • Property‑buyer mail: an agent drafts a property‑specific note per record; a service like Lob or Thanks.io prints and mails it for ~$1–3/piece — cheaper and better‑fit than Sendoso, which is built for corporate gifting, not one‑page personal letters at this volume
  • Realtor BD outreach once the MLS‑sourced list exists: email + LinkedIn, focused on the ~150–200 most active land/teardown realtors, not all 500
  • Phone follow‑up only on warm responders — never cold dialing first
4
Ongoing · Inbound & Feedback

Compounding channels + close the loop

  • Houzz Pro profile, Google Ads, Meta/Instagram — budgets and expected volume in Sheet 3
  • Referral flywheel: every closed job becomes a review and a referral ask
  • Track which channel produces closed jobs, not just leads, and reallocate monthly
5
Maturity · Competitive Intel & Autonomous Loop

Watch the market, run the loop continuously

  • Monitor competitor positioning via the free Meta Ad Library and a tool like SpyFu/SEMrush for competitor Google Ads keywords — see what other Twin Cities design/build firms run, and where the gap is
  • Stand up a standing agent that re‑pulls the county feeds weekly, re‑scores new signals, drafts outreach copy for new records, and only surfaces a human decision for anything that costs money or gets sent
  • Shift channel budget automatically toward whatever is actually converting to closed jobs
